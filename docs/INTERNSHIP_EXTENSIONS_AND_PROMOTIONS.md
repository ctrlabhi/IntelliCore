# Internship extensions and promotions

Completion sign-off, extension, promotion and recording external certificate email are independent decisions. The primary Founder and authorized HR Managers manage completion follow-ups. HR Executives and Team Leaders have read access within their assigned scope. Interns see their own history, including completed periods.

## Extension

Enter a start date, either an end date or duration in calendar days, a reason and optional remarks. Date boundaries follow Asia/Kolkata. A duration includes the first day. An open-period extension begins the day after the original expected end and records both expected end dates. An already completed period remains immutable. Its extension creates a separate continuation linked to the approved source period and decision, with its own future eligibility and manual completion. Existing attendance, reports, tasks, evaluations and learning remain with their original periods. A gap does not create attendance or active workspace eligibility.

Concurrent operations use the intern's user-row lock, shared by the application and enrollment flows. The extension path checks overlapping open and previously completed periods. A future unused extension can be cancelled with a reason. Cancellation preserves history and removes the future eligibility. A later extension does not inherit the original period's declared certificate email status.

## Promotion

HR can record a non-privileged intern designation with an effective date and reason. The approved designation can take effect on an eligible continuation. Completed titles and role grants remain historical. A recommendation for a supported staff role includes a department, designation, reason and eligible joining date. It has separate statuses for pending review, approved, rejected, cancelled and provisioned.

The primary Founder reviews recommendations submitted by another actor and may provision approved candidates on or after the effective date with a recently authenticated session. Activation uses the existing role, staff and department services in the same transaction as the promotion record and audits. Approval alone grants no workspace access. An existing active staff member follows the protected staff update and role assignment path. Active internship or application conflicts and restricted accounts block staff provisioning.
