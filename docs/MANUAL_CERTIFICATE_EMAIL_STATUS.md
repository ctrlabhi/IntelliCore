# Manual certificate email status

The owner creates, stores and sends certificates outside IntelliCore. After an email has already been sent externally, the primary Founder or an authorized HR Manager can open the completed internship, declare the sent time and confirm the external send. The status records the team declaration and timestamps. There is no independent inbox measurement.

The status starts as **Not marked as sent**. A successful declaration changes it to **Sent manually** and writes exactly one in-app notification to the intern with the owner-approved message.

> Your internship certificate has been sent to your registered email address. Please check your inbox and spam folder.

The internship timeline shows the current state and the notification center displays the notice. Authorized HR Executives can read status and history. An intern can only read their own status. An incorrect declaration can be corrected with a reason. The original event is retained, the notice is marked superseded, and a new in-app correction points the intern to their current status. A subsequent declaration requires a new operation key and creates a new history entry.

The application stores only the enrollment, declaring actor, timestamps, status, request key and a bounded note or correction reason. PDF generation, identifiers, uploads/downloads, certificate metadata, public verification, automatic email and any certificate outbox jobs are outside this workflow. Existing non-certificate completion emails continue under Phase 07 policy. Historical template assets remain inactive.
